History

Macy's started the Thanksgiving Day Parade in 1924 to boost holiday sales and celebrate the opening of its newly expanded Herald Square store. Employees, many of them European immigrants, wanted to create a festive celebration like those in their home countries and to kick off the Christmas shopping season, which also helped them compete with rival department store Gimbel's. The first parade, initially called the Macy's Christmas Parade, featured employees in costume, floats, and animals on loan from the Central Park Zoo.
